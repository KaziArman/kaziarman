### About me

- 🏢 **Industry:** Advanced Analytics Intern, Data Research and Analytics, **AAA, The Auto Club Group**. I build conversational data agents (Google ADK, Gemini) that answer business questions over BigQuery and Tableau, and an evaluation framework that catches hallucinated answers before deployment.
- 🔬 **Research:** M.S. in Industrial Engineering and Management (Operations Research and Analytics), **Oklahoma State University**. My thesis develops privacy-preserving decentralized stochastic optimization for power grids and electrified chemical plants, in the [DISys Lab](https://disys-lab.github.io/) with Dr. Paritosh Ramanan.
- 🎓 **Now:** completing an M.S. in Business Analytics and Data Science (4.0 GPA).
- 🏆 National Champion, Data4Good Competition (Johns Hopkins University) · Finalist, IISE Energy Analytics Challenge 2026

### What I work on

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Decentralized optimization** | ADMM-based coordination of a 2,000-bus grid and 26 chemical plants that never share private operating data |
| **Stochastic programming** | Two-stage reserve planning under renewable uncertainty, with scenarios generated without pooling data |
| **Federated learning** | Load forecasting across plants, including cold-start transfer for a newly connected plant |
| **Agentic AI** | LLM agents for analytics, and agents that tune optimization algorithms on their own |
